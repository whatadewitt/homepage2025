Twenty seasons in, the Freddy Beach Fantasy Baseball League needed a proper
home. This is it:

- **The constitution.** Every rule, from the auction draft and salary cap to
  the draft punishment (chips, beer and exactly the right brand of dip) and the
  Doug "Anti-Slime" rules.
- **Custom stats.** MASH (Matchup Adjusted Simulated Head-to-head) ranks every
  team against the whole league each week, not just its opponent. There are roto
  standings too, and Taters, a home run tracker with a weekly Homer Cup.
- **Tools.** A trade calculator, and a ledger of who owes dip for which rule
  infraction.
- **A blog.** Weekly recaps through the season. In 2026 we ran into problems
  with Yahoo! mid-season, so the blog cuts off at week 17.

It's built with Astro and pulls league data from Yahoo! with my own
[Yahoo! Fantasy Sports API client](/projects/yahoo-fantasy-sports-api).
