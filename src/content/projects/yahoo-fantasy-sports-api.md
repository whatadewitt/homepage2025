A labour of love I've been working on for years. This open-source NodeJS module
wraps the Yahoo! Fantasy Sports API and lets you work with leagues, teams and
player stats.

The [docs site](https://y-fantasy-node-docs.vercel.app/) is fully
interactive: authenticate and make calls against your own league data, right
from the page.
