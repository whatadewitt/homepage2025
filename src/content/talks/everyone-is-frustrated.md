If you're early in your career, the market feels like a wall: applications
disappear, "entry-level" postings ask for three years, and nobody tells you why
you were passed over.

This panel put both sides in the same room, the candidates and the people
screening them, to get specific about how hiring decisions actually get made and
which candidates the process is filtering out.

**On the panel:** Joel Varty (Agility CMS), Bermon Painter (Slalom), Brian Holt
(Microsoft) and me.
